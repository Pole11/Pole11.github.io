# An Hour a Day for Myself?
## Why do I run?

Nice question, thanks for asking.

For a long time, I dedicated almost 100% of my available time to studying. Although I genuinely loved what I was learning, I later realised that having so little variety in my daily routine was holding me back me from fully enjoying it.

So, I decided to make room for some of my other passions (interests that, until then, I hadn't given enough attention to).

One of them was sim racing. Well, sim racing without a sim, to be precise! 
When I realised that I didn't actually need a sophisticated setup to enjoy it, I started playing Assetto Corsa with my Xbox 360 controller. I made myself a simple promise: one hour a day, just for me.

It certainly helped me create some balance in my routine, but I had no idea that something else would eventually make an even bigger difference.

One day, a friend of mine (a real athlete) invited me to go for a run. I still don't know exactly why, but I said yes.

We ran approximately 3 km. By the end of it, I genuinely felt like I was dying. My heart rate was probably somewhere above 200 bpm!

And yet, I loved it.

There was something special about the experience. It felt like facing my fears, deliberately stepping outside my comfort zone, and choosing to embrace the discomfort. Even better, I was doing it alongside a friend.

That day changed the way I looked at spending time on myself. I realised that sometimes the most rewarding way to spend an hour is not necessarily the most comfortable one.

I have become convinced that there is a lot to learn from people who choose to dedicate an hour of their day to challenging themselves, rather than simply taking the easiest option and spending that time on the sofa.

And perhaps the most important lesson is that you don't have to be good at something to enjoy it. You just have to be willing to start.
